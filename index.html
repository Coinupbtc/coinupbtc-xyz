The lab

Four things I measured or fixed on my own hardware, cut down until each one fits in a single file you can read. No backend, no framework, no build step, no tracking — every demo runs entirely in your browser, and the last one measures your machine, not mine.

These are not screenshots of tools. They are the actual mechanisms, reimplemented small: the fee-band routing behind a mempool visualiser, the arithmetic that decides how many tokens a draft model should guess, the render bug that made a click take 13 seconds, and the interference pattern used as the motif on both sites.

  • 01 Fee-band router Transactions arrive, get sorted into fee bands, and race for space in the next few blocks. Set your own fee rate and watch which block — if any — takes you. canvas · simulated mempool
  • 02 Speculative decoding A small model guesses ahead, a big model checks its work. Drag the draft length and the acceptance rate to find where the speedup peaks — and where guessing more starts costing you. the curve has a maximum
  • 03 13s → 7.5ms A real render bug, reproduced. 516 rows, one click, two implementations. Your browser times both and prints the gap — no numbers of mine involved. measures your machine
  • 04 Moiré bench Two grids, slightly disagreeing. Rotate and detune them to raise interference patterns out of nothing — including the exact motif behind the header above. canvas · pure geometry
