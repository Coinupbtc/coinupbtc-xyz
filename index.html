Play

Four things to play with

  • Live data

    Blockfield

    The Bitcoin mempool as a block war: the next three blocks fighting for fee bands, live, with inscriptions floating through. Refreshes itself; nothing to configure.

    Live Reads mempool.space every few seconds. The purge floor, hashrate and halving countdown are the real numbers right now.

    Watch it →

  • Toy

    Falling sand

    Draw with sand, water, stone, wood and fire and let them react. Drop little people in and build them a bridge. A chemistry set with no cleanup.

    Try Pick 👤 Person, draw a floor, then light a fire under the wood.

    Play it →

  • Game

    Gravity lander

    Land on the moon from your phone. Thrust is a thumb, the fuel is real, and the ground is less forgiving than it looks.

    Try Built phone-first in Three.js; works in landscape and portrait.

    Fly it →

  • Measurement

    Moiré bench

    Two grids that almost line up. The pattern you see is where they disagree. A vernier caliper reads small differences the same way: a tiny mismatch between two scales becomes easy to see.

    Try Move one layer’s spacing by a single 1% step and watch the pattern shift a long way.

    Open the demo →

The bench

Four things measured

  • Performance

    Render cost

    Selecting one row used to rebuild every row in the list. This runs the bug and the fix side by side and times both in your browser.

    Measured In the real app: 13 s before the fix, 7.5 ms after, at 516 records.

    Open the demo →

  • AI serving

    Speculative decoding

    A small model drafts the next few words and the big model checks them all in one pass. Move the sliders to see when drafting saves time and when it costs time.

    Measured On a copy-heavy refactor, 34.33 → 74.48 tokens per second. Report §9

    Open the demo →

  • Capacity planning

    Memory packer

    Pick one, two or three DGX Spark machines and the models you want to run, and see whether they fit. Each machine has its own memory; two machines are not one big pool.

    Measured 121 GiB per machine; the video model holds 89.42 GiB on one after loading. Results

    Open the demo →

  • Agent efficiency

    Context carry

    Every agent step re-reads the whole session, every tool result included. Watch old tool output pile up step by step, then turn on clearing it and see what that saves.

    Measured On my agent, 50.8% of everything read was old tool output, each token re-read 54×. The audit
